Program Overview

The Mozaiq Future Academy Drone Racing program introduces students to drone technology, flight fundamentals, safe piloting, obstacle navigation and the excitement of friendly drone racing. The curriculum combines structured technical instruction with short, engaging games so students remain active, motivated and involved throughout every session.

Program Objectives

  • Understand the basic components of a drone and the principles of flight.
  • Apply safe pre-flight, in-flight and post-flight procedures.
  • Develop basic control skills, including hovering, directional movement, turning and landing.
  • Improve hand-eye coordination, spatial awareness, precision and reaction time.
  • Navigate cones, hoops and racing gates using controlled flight paths.
  • Understand the fundamentals of FPV technology and simulator-based racing.
  • Demonstrate teamwork, sportsmanship, responsibility and problem-solving.

Performance Levels

  • Level 1 — Beginner: Requires frequent instructor guidance and support.
  • Level 2 — Developing: Performs basic operations with occasional guidance.
  • Level 3 — Competent: Completes familiar drills and simple obstacle routes independently within safety rules.
  • Level 4 — Advanced: Demonstrates consistent precision, sound judgement and strong racing skills.

Expected Learning Outcomes

  • Identify the main components of a beginner drone and explain basic flight concepts.
  • Follow pre-flight and post-flight safety procedures.
  • Perform controlled take-off, hovering, directional movement and landing with suitable equipment.
  • Navigate beginner slalom and gate courses at a controlled speed.
  • Demonstrate basic understanding of race timing, scoring and fair play.
  • Explain the purpose of FPV technology and experience simulator-based racing.
  • Contribute positively to team challenges and demonstrate responsibility and sportsmanship.
