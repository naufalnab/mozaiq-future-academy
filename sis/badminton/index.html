Program Overview

The Mozaiq Badminton Academy program is designed to provide students with an exciting, engaging, and progressive badminton learning experience. The curriculum combines technical skill development, physical coordination, tactical awareness, teamwork, and friendly competition. Each session features a different theme and fun challenges to keep students motivated and actively involved.

Program Objectives

  • Develop fundamental badminton skills and confidence.
  • Improve agility, balance, coordination, and general physical fitness.
  • Introduce basic singles and doubles strategies.
  • Encourage teamwork, communication, discipline, and sportsmanship.
  • Make every session enjoyable through themed games and challenges.

10-Session SyllabusSilabus 10 Sesi

01Welcome to Badminton!

Theme: Introduction & Basic Skills

Technical Training (27 minutes)

  • Introduction to badminton equipment and court layout.
  • Basic racket grip: forehand and backhand.
  • Ready position and basic racket control.
  • Hand-eye coordination exercises.

Fun Games (28 minutes)

  • Shuttlecock Balance Challenge
  • Hit the Target
  • Racket & Shuttlecock Relay

Learning Outcome: Students understand basic badminton equipment, develop racket familiarity, and confidently attempt their first shots.

02Footwork Ninja Challenge

Theme: Agility & Movement

Technical Training (27 minutes)

  • Basic ready position.
  • Side steps and forward-backward movement.
  • Four-corner footwork patterns.
  • Recovery steps and court positioning.

Fun Games (28 minutes)

  • Badminton Traffic Light
  • Four Corners Ninja
  • Shuttlecock Treasure Hunt

Learning Outcome: Students improve agility, balance, coordination, and movement around the badminton court.

03Serve Like a Champion

Theme: Serving Techniques

Technical Training (27 minutes)

  • Introduction to forehand and backhand serves.
  • Basic short serve technique.
  • Basic long serve technique.
  • Shuttlecock direction and accuracy.

Fun Games (28 minutes)

  • Serve to the Zone
  • Target Service Challenge
  • King of the Serve

Learning Outcome: Students can perform basic serves with improved control, accuracy, and consistency.

04The Power of the Clear

Theme: Forehand & Backhand Strokes

Technical Training (27 minutes)

  • Introduction to the forehand clear.
  • Basic backhand technique.
  • Overhead hitting movement.
  • Contact point and follow-through.

Fun Games (28 minutes)

  • Long Distance Challenge
  • Deep Corner Target
  • Clear the Castle

Learning Outcome: Students develop basic overhead hitting skills and learn to send the shuttlecock towards the back of the court.

05Net Master Challenge

Theme: Net Play & Control

Technical Training (27 minutes)

  • Basic net shot techniques.
  • Racket touch and shuttlecock control.
  • Introduction to the net lift.
  • Foot positioning near the net.

Fun Games (28 minutes)

  • Net Precision Challenge
  • Drop & Catch Challenge
  • Net Battle 1 vs. 1

Learning Outcome: Students improve touch, precision, and control when playing near the front of the court.

06Smash Attack Academy

Theme: Attacking & Defensive Skills

Technical Training (27 minutes)

  • Introduction to the badminton smash.
  • Timing and contact point.
  • Basic attacking movement.
  • Defensive ready position.

Fun Games (28 minutes)

  • Smash Target Challenge
  • Attack vs. Defense
  • Shuttlecock Speed Challenge

Learning Outcome: Students understand basic attacking and defensive techniques and learn when to use them during a rally.

07Doubles Teamwork Challenge

Theme: Teamwork & Communication

Technical Training (27 minutes)

  • Introduction to doubles positioning.
  • Front-and-back formation.
  • Side-by-side formation.
  • Communication and partner coordination.

Fun Games (28 minutes)

  • Partner Rally Challenge
  • Team Shuttle Relay
  • Doubles Rotation Game

Learning Outcome: Students understand basic doubles positioning, communication, and teamwork.

08Badminton Tactical Mission

Theme: Game Strategy & Decision Making

Technical Training (27 minutes)

  • Introduction to singles strategy.
  • Reading the opponent's position.
  • Hitting into open spaces.
  • Rally consistency and shot selection.

Fun Games (28 minutes)

  • Find the Empty Space
  • 10-Shot Rally Challenge
  • Tactical Mission 1 vs. 1

Learning Outcome: Students develop basic tactical awareness and learn to make simple decisions during a badminton game.

09Mini Badminton Olympics

Theme: Competition & Skills Assessment

Technical Training (27 minutes)

  • Review of fundamental badminton techniques.
  • Rules and scoring system.
  • Match preparation and warm-up.
  • Basic competition strategies.

Fun Games (28 minutes)

  • Badminton Skills Olympics
  • Singles Mini Tournament
  • Doubles Team Competition

Learning Outcome: Students apply their badminton skills in friendly competition while demonstrating confidence and sportsmanship.

10Badminton Championship & Graduation

Theme: Final Tournament & Celebration

Technical Training (27 minutes)

  • Final technical skill assessment.
  • Review of footwork, serving, and rally techniques.
  • Sportsmanship and fair-play principles.
  • Match preparation.

Fun Games (28 minutes)

  • Champion Challenge
  • Singles & Doubles Championship
  • Team Celebration & Award Ceremony

Learning Outcome: Students demonstrate their progress, confidence, technical skills, and understanding of badminton through a final friendly championship.

Safety Guidelines

  1. All students must complete warm-up exercises before training.
  2. Maintain sufficient distance between students when swinging badminton rackets.
  3. Avoid swinging rackets too close to other participants.
  4. Students should collect shuttlecocks only when instructed by the coach.
  5. Appropriate indoor sports shoes must be worn.
  6. Training intensity and activities must be adjusted according to students' ages and skill levels.
  7. Coaches must supervise all activities and ensure that first aid arrangements are available.
