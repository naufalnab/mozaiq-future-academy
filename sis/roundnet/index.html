Program Overview

Roundnet is a team sport played around a low, circular net placed close to the ground. Two teams of two players take turns hitting a ball onto the net so that it rebounds toward the opposing team and becomes difficult to return. This program introduces students to the fundamental skills and rules of Roundnet, progressing toward 2 vs 2 match play through active, enjoyable, and age-appropriate learning activities.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand Roundnet equipment, the playing area, basic rules, and safety procedures.
  • Develop hand-eye coordination, ball control, accuracy, and reaction speed.
  • Learn the fundamental techniques of serving, passing, receiving, and attacking.
  • Understand teamwork, communication, positioning, and basic game strategies.
  • Apply the rules and scoring system during 2 vs 2 games.
  • Demonstrate sportsmanship, confidence, discipline, and respect for others.

The coach will use a game-based learning approach to keep students active and minimise waiting time. Activities should be adapted to students' ages, abilities, group size, and available equipment.

Safety Guidelines

  • Ensure the playing area is level, dry, clean, and free from objects that may cause injury.
  • Check the stability of the frame and the condition of the net before each session.
  • Conduct age- and ability-appropriate warm-up and cool-down activities.
  • Maintain safe distances between courts and prevent students from crossing active playing areas.
  • Teach controlled movement first; avoid diving or high-risk falling movements for beginners.
  • Use balls and equipment that are in good condition and appropriate for the activity.
  • Provide drinking water, rest breaks, and a first aid kit.
  • The coach must stop activities if the playing surface or weather conditions become unsafe.

Expected Program Outcomes

By the end of the 10-session program, students are expected to:

  • Explain the objective of Roundnet, identify the equipment, and describe the basic rules.
  • Perform basic serving, passing, receiving, and attacking techniques.
  • Understand teamwork and the three-touch sequence within a team's possession.
  • Participate in 2 vs 2 matches and understand the scoring system.
  • Demonstrate communication, sportsmanship, confidence, and respect for others.
