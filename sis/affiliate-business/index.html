Program Description

Affiliate Business Academy is a digital entrepreneurship program that introduces students to affiliate marketing through Shopee and TikTok. Students explore product research, target audiences, personal branding, content creation, copywriting, promotional strategies, affiliate links, performance analytics, and commission simulations.

The program uses project-based learning and short, engaging challenges to encourage creativity, collaboration, and practical experience. Its primary focus is building digital business skills and understanding commission-based earning models—not guaranteeing a particular income.

Final Project — My First Affiliate Business

Each student or group prepares a final project portfolio containing:

  • Brand concept: name, niche, target audience, and bio.
  • Product research portfolio: at least 5 suitable products from age-appropriate and safe categories.
  • Promotional content: at least 3 storyboards or short videos.
  • Copywriting: at least 2 captions and calls to action.
  • Campaign plan: promotional flow and distribution channels.
  • Income simulation: commission target, assumed commission per transaction, and required eligible transactions.
  • Reflection: key learning points and proposed improvements.

Digital Ethics, Safety & Platform Compliance

  • Students must create original content and respect copyright for music, images, videos, and third-party materials.
  • Product information must be truthful; students must not make claims they cannot substantiate.
  • Affiliate promotions must include clear and appropriate disclosure in accordance with platform rules.
  • Do not promote illegal, dangerous, age-restricted, or school-prohibited products.
  • No fake purchases, artificial clicks, metric manipulation, spam, or transactions intended to generate illegitimate commissions.
  • Personal data, phone numbers, addresses, passwords, bank details, and payment information must not be shared in class or in content.
  • Public accounts, affiliate registration, and content publication require appropriate school/parental consent and compliance with current platform age and account requirements.
  • Students who do not meet platform age requirements will use simulations, instructor demonstrations, and non-monetized projects. They must not misrepresent their age or use another person's identity.
