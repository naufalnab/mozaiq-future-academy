Program Overview

The Mozaiq Pickleball Academy curriculum is designed to introduce students to pickleball through a progressive, engaging, and age-appropriate learning experience. The program combines technical instruction, guided practice, movement activities, teamwork, and modified games so that students remain active and motivated throughout each session.

Standard Session Structure

Each meeting follows a consistent 55-minute structure. Coaches may adjust individual drill durations to suit group size, court availability, and student ability while maintaining the overall 50/50 balance.

10-Session SyllabusSilabus 10 Sesi

01Welcome to Pickleball!

Focus: Introduction, equipment, grip and ball control

Learning objectives

  • Identify basic equipment and court areas.
  • Understand essential safety expectations and introductory rules.
  • Develop paddle awareness and hand-eye coordination.

15:15–15:42 | Drilling & theory (27 minutes)

  • 15:15–15:20 (5 min): Dynamic warm-up and movement game.
  • 15:20–15:25 (5 min): Introduce paddles, balls, court layout and basic rules.
  • 15:25–15:32 (7 min): Demonstrate ready position and comfortable paddle grip.
  • 15:32–15:42 (10 min): Individual ball bounce, balance and paddle-control progressions.

15:42–16:10 | Games & application (28 minutes)

  • 15:42–15:50 (8 min): Paddle Balance Challenge.
  • 15:50–15:59 (9 min): Keep the Ball Alive – individual or partner version.
  • 15:59–16:07 (8 min): Target Hitting Challenge.
  • 16:07–16:10 (3 min): Cool-down and one learning takeaway.

Expected outcome: Students can hold the paddle safely, adopt a ready position and control a ball with basic consistency.

02Forehand & Backhand Foundations

Focus: Groundstroke technique and directional control

Learning objectives

  • Recognize forehand and backhand contact positions.
  • Use a balanced stance and controlled swing.
  • Direct a ball toward a large target area.

15:15–15:42 | Drilling & theory (27 minutes)

  • 15:15–15:20 (5 min): Dynamic warm-up and shadow swings.
  • 15:20–15:25 (5 min): Explain contact point, paddle face and follow-through.
  • 15:25–15:32 (7 min): Coach-fed forehand practice at a suitable distance.
  • 15:32–15:37 (5 min): Backhand demonstration and key cues.
  • 15:37–15:42 (5 min): Partner backhand feeds and controlled returns.

15:42–16:10 | Games & application (28 minutes)

  • 15:42–15:51 (9 min): Forehand Target Challenge.
  • 15:51–16:00 (9 min): Backhand Accuracy Battle.
  • 16:00–16:07 (7 min): King/Queen of the Court – cooperative or points-based version.
  • 16:07–16:10 (3 min): Cool-down and feedback.

Expected outcome: Students can attempt basic forehand and backhand groundstrokes with improved balance and control.

03Serve Like a Champion

Focus: Legal underhand serve and serve placement

Learning objectives

  • Understand the basic underhand serve requirement.
  • Develop a repeatable serving routine.
  • Aim serves into a designated service box.

15:15–15:42 | Drilling & theory (27 minutes)

  • 15:15–15:20 (5 min): Warm-up and footwork activation.
  • 15:20–15:27 (7 min): Explain serving rules, stance and contact; demonstrate a legal serve.
  • 15:27–15:37 (10 min): Individual serving practice from a manageable distance.
  • 15:37–15:42 (5 min): Serve placement drills using target zones.

15:42–16:10 | Games & application (28 minutes)

  • 15:42–15:51 (9 min): Serve to the Zones.
  • 15:51–16:00 (9 min): Three-Target Serving Challenge.
  • 16:00–16:07 (7 min): Serve and Score – short rounds.
  • 16:07–16:10 (3 min): Cool-down and review.

Expected outcome: Students can attempt a legal underhand serve and direct it toward a chosen area.

04Net Attack & Volley Skills

Focus: Volley technique, reaction and safe net play

Learning objectives

  • Understand when a volley is permitted and the Non-Volley Zone restriction.
  • Use a stable ready position and compact volley motion.
  • Improve reaction and paddle preparation.

15:15–15:42 | Drilling & theory (27 minutes)

  • 15:15–15:20 (5 min): Reaction warm-up.
  • 15:20–15:27 (7 min): Demonstrate volley technique, ready position and Non-Volley Zone rules.
  • 15:27–15:35 (8 min): Partner or coach-fed controlled volley drills.
  • 15:35–15:42 (7 min): Reaction and directional movement activities.

15:42–16:10 | Games & application (28 minutes)

  • 15:42–15:51 (9 min): Volley Rally Challenge.
  • 15:51–16:00 (9 min): Fast Hands – controlled partner challenge.
  • 16:00–16:07 (7 min): Net Battle – first to 7, modified as needed.
  • 16:07–16:10 (3 min): Cool-down and reflection.

Expected outcome: Students can perform a basic controlled volley and demonstrate awareness of the Non-Volley Zone.

05Mastering the Kitchen Zone

Focus: Dinking, touch and court awareness

Learning objectives

  • Identify the Non-Volley Zone (Kitchen).
  • Understand the key restriction on volleying while in the zone.
  • Practice soft, controlled dink shots.

15:15–15:42 | Drilling & theory (27 minutes)

  • 15:15–15:20 (5 min): Warm-up and coordination activity.
  • 15:20–15:27 (7 min): Explain Kitchen boundaries and volley restriction.
  • 15:27–15:37 (10 min): Demonstrate and practice a soft dink over the net.
  • 15:37–15:42 (5 min): Partner dink rally with a focus on control.

15:42–16:10 | Games & application (28 minutes)

  • 15:42–15:51 (9 min): Dink Rally Challenge.
  • 15:51–16:00 (9 min): Kitchen Target Challenge.
  • 16:00–16:07 (7 min): Dink Battle – cooperative or first-to-7 format.
  • 16:07–16:10 (3 min): Cool-down and key rule recap.

Expected outcome: Students can attempt controlled dink shots and explain the basic Kitchen volley restriction.

  • 15:20–15:27 (7 min): Explain doubles rotation, positioning and communication cues.

15:42–16:10 | Games & application (28 minutes)

  • 15:25–15:35 (10 min): Combination drills – serve, return and rally.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of the 10-session program, students should be able to demonstrate progress in the following areas:

  • Technical skills: adopt a safe ready position; control the ball; attempt forehand and backhand groundstrokes; serve underhand; perform basic volleys and dinks; and sustain a simple rally.
  • Rules and game understanding: identify key court areas, understand the Non-Volley Zone restriction, follow basic serving and scoring procedures, and participate in singles or doubles activities.
  • Physical development: improve hand-eye coordination, balance, agility, reaction time and movement around the court.
  • Social and personal development: communicate with partners, cooperate in teams, show confidence, respect opponents, and demonstrate fair play.

Safety Guidelines

  • Conduct a brief safety check of the playing area before each session; do not use a wet, slippery or obstructed surface.
  • Begin with an appropriate warm-up and finish with a cool-down.
  • Students must wear suitable sports shoes and comfortable sports clothing.
  • Maintain safe spacing between students during paddle swings and practice activities.
  • Do not swing paddles near another student; collect loose balls only when the coach signals that play has stopped.
  • Keep bags, bottles and unused equipment outside the playing area.
  • Teach and reinforce the Non-Volley Zone rule; do not encourage unsafe lunging or collisions near the net.
  • Ensure hydration breaks are available, especially in hot or humid conditions.
  • Students must inform the coach or school staff if they feel unwell, dizzy or injured.
  • Follow SIS safeguarding, supervision, emergency and incident-reporting procedures at all times.
