Program Concept

Handball Academy introduces students to the fundamentals of team handball through an active, inclusive and enjoyable learning experience. Each session combines technical instruction with short challenges and modified games, allowing students to practise skills in realistic playing situations while remaining engaged and motivated.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand basic handball rules, court awareness and safe participation.
  • Develop fundamental skills: passing, catching, dribbling, footwork and shooting.
  • Apply simple attacking and defensive principles in small-sided activities.
  • Improve coordination, agility, balance, decision-making and spatial awareness.
  • Demonstrate teamwork, communication, confidence, respect and sportsmanship.

Session Structure

ComponentTime allocationFocus
Drilling + theory27–28 minutes (approx. 50%)Brief explanation, demonstration, skill practice and feedback.
Games / challenges27–28 minutes (approx. 50%)Fun games, skill challenges, team competitions and mini-match play.

Timing note: Each session lasts 55 minutes. The two learning components are divided as evenly as possible. A brief cool-down and reflection are integrated at the end so the overall balance remains close to 50:50.

01Introduction To Handball

Session learning outcome: Students become familiar with the ball, basic rules, safe movement, and the purpose of passing and catching.

TIMEACTIVITY

15:20–15:27Introduction to handball: objective, basic court areas, ball grip and simple rules.
