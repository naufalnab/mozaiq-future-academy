Program Rationale

Ultimate Frisbee is a non-contact team sport played with a flying disc. Teams advance the disc by passing to teammates and score by completing a catch in the opposing end zone. The sport develops throwing and catching skills, agility, spatial awareness, communication, decision-making, and teamwork. Its distinctive Spirit of the Game principle encourages honesty, mutual respect, self-officiation, and fair play.

This 10-session curriculum is designed for school-age learners and progresses from introductory skills to tactical understanding and modified match play. Activities are adapted to student age, ability, group size, weather, and the available SIS playing area.

Program Objectives

  • Understand the basic concept, equipment, safety expectations, and rules of Ultimate Frisbee.
  • Demonstrate introductory backhand and forehand throws.
  • Catch a disc using safe and effective two-hand catching techniques.
  • Apply pivoting, balance, and movement principles while in possession of the disc.
  • Move into open space and support teammates without the disc.
  • Apply introductory marking and non-contact defensive positioning.
  • Use communication and simple team strategies to maintain possession and create scoring opportunities.
  • Participate in modified games while demonstrating respect, honesty, and sportsmanship.

Safety Guidelines

  • Conduct a venue and surface check before every session; remove obstacles and identify unsafe or slippery areas.
  • Begin with an appropriate warm-up and finish with a brief cool-down.
  • Use age-appropriate discs and adjust throwing distance, speed, and activity complexity to student ability.
  • Do not throw discs towards a person’s face or head; players should retrieve discs only when the area is clear.
  • Maintain safe spacing between throwing lanes and practice stations.
  • Ultimate Frisbee is a non-contact sport: no pushing, holding, blocking by contact, or intentional body contact.
  • Defenders must maintain control and avoid dangerous bids or collisions; stop play if a student is at risk.
  • Ensure students have suitable sports footwear, access to drinking water, and breaks when required by heat or exertion.
  • The coach must supervise all stations and games and have access to the school’s emergency and first-aid procedures.
  • Stop activity immediately in the event of injury, unsafe weather, poor visibility, or an unsafe playing surface.

Program Completion Outcomes

By the end of the 10-session programme, students should be able to:

  • Perform introductory backhand and forehand throws with improved control.
  • Catch a flying disc safely and consistently at an age-appropriate level.
  • Use a pivot and move into open space to support teammates.
  • Apply simple offensive and defensive principles in small-sided games.
  • Understand basic scoring, turnovers, and key rules of modified Ultimate Frisbee.
  • Participate positively in team play and demonstrate the Spirit of the Game.
