Program Objectives

The Mozaiq Future Academy Pingpong Program introduces and develops students’ table tennis skills through progressive technical learning, focused drills, skill challenges, and enjoyable games. The program emphasizes active participation, safety, and individual student development.

  • Understand table tennis equipment, basic rules, and playing etiquette.
  • Develop paddle grip, ready position, and basic forehand and backhand strokes.
  • Improve footwork, hand-eye coordination, and ball control.
  • Practice serving, receiving, and sustaining rallies.
  • Explore spin, topspin, blocking, and simple game strategies.
  • Build confidence, discipline, teamwork, and sportsmanship.

Standard Session Structure

Each session runs for 55 minutes, from 15:15 to 16:10. Time is divided equally between technical learning and game-based activities.

Safety Guidelines

  • The coach checks tables, nets, flooring, and the training area before each session.
  • Students complete a warm-up and wear suitable indoor sports shoes.
  • Maintain safe spacing between tables; never swing a paddle toward another student.
  • Students retrieve balls only when the playing area is safe and the coach permits it.
  • No running around tables, pushing, or horseplay while holding a paddle.
  • Use balls and paddles as instructed; never throw equipment.
  • Provide access to drinking water and rest breaks as needed.
  • Any injury, pain, or discomfort must be reported to the coach immediately.
  • The coach supervises all activities and adjusts intensity to students’ age and ability.
