Program Overview

Mozaiq Padel Academy introduces students to padel through an enjoyable, interactive, safe, and progressive learning experience. Each session combines concise theory, coach demonstrations, technical practice, and engaging games that help students apply newly learned skills.

Program Objectives

  • Understand the basic rules, etiquette, equipment, and scoring system of padel.
  • Develop the fundamentals of racket grip, ready position, forehand, and backhand strokes.
  • Learn the underhand serve and basic return techniques.
  • Understand glass-wall rebounds and safe wall-play techniques.
  • Develop basic volley, footwork, and court-positioning skills.
  • Build communication, teamwork, and basic doubles strategies.
  • Participate in rallies, mini matches, and friendly competitions.

Standard Session Structure

Each session lasts 55 minutes and follows an equal 50:50 learning and play structure.

Safety Guidelines

  1. Students must wear sports shoes suitable for the padel-court surface.
  2. A warm-up must be completed before racket drills and games.
  3. Rackets must be appropriate for each student's age, size, and ability.
  4. Students must maintain a safe distance when swinging their rackets.
  5. Students must not enter another group's hitting area without the coach's instruction.
  6. Students must never intentionally hit the ball towards another person's body.
  7. Glass-wall play must be introduced according to the coach's instructions and student ability.
  8. Students must not push, climb on, or collide with glass walls.
  9. Coaches must provide hydration breaks and monitor student wellbeing.
  10. All activities must take place under coach supervision.
