Program Overview

Floorball is a fast-paced indoor team sport played with lightweight sticks and a lightweight plastic ball with holes. Players work together to move the ball, create scoring opportunities, and score in the opposing goal. The program introduces students to the sport through progressive skill development, teamwork, movement, tactical awareness, and supervised small-sided games.

10-Session SyllabusSilabus 10 Sesi

01Introduction to Floorball

Technical Drilling & Theory (15:15–15:42): 15:15–15:20 Dynamic warm-up; 15:20–15:27 introduction to equipment, playing area and basic rules; 15:27–15:42 stick grip, ready position and basic ball control.

Games & Practical Application (15:42–16:10): 15:42–15:50 stick-and-ball familiarization challenge; 15:50–16:02 individual ball-control race; 16:02–16:10 team relay challenge.

Session outcome: Recognize equipment and demonstrate a safe ready position and basic ball control.

02Stick Handling & Ball Control

Technical Drilling & Theory (15:15–15:42): 15:15–15:20 warm-up and coordination; 15:20–15:30 forehand and backhand control; 15:30–15:42 straight-line and cone dribbling.

Games & Practical Application (15:42–16:10): 15:42–15:52 dribbling obstacle challenge; 15:52–16:05 1-vs-1 ball-protection game; 16:05–16:10 skill challenge.

Session outcome: Dribble with control while changing direction and protecting the ball.

03Passing & Receiving

Technical Drilling & Theory (15:15–15:42): 15:15–15:20 warm-up; 15:20–15:30 push-pass technique and ball position; 15:30–15:42 receiving, cushioning and controlling passes.

Games & Practical Application (15:42–16:10): 15:42–15:52 partner passing challenge; 15:52–16:02 passing-accuracy competition; 16:02–16:10 3-vs-3 possession game.

Session outcome: Perform basic push passes and receive a pass with control.

04Shooting & Scoring

Technical Drilling & Theory (15:15–15:42): 15:15–15:20 warm-up; 15:20–15:30 basic push-shot and wrist-shot introduction; 15:30–15:42 shooting accuracy and target practice.

Games & Practical Application (15:42–16:10): 15:42–15:52 shooting-accuracy competition; 15:52–16:02 scoring challenge; 16:02–16:10 mini shooting competition.

Session outcome: Demonstrate a safe basic shot and aim toward a target.

05Offensive Skills & Attacking Movement

Technical Drilling & Theory (15:15–15:42): 15:15–15:20 warm-up; 15:20–15:30 creating space and moving without the ball; 15:30–15:42 pass-and-move attacking combinations.

Games & Practical Application (15:42–16:10): 15:42–15:52 2-vs-1 attacking challenge; 15:52–16:05 3-vs-3 attacking game; 16:05–16:10 team scoring challenge.

Session outcome: Move into open space and support an attacking teammate.

06Defensive Skills & Teamwork

Technical Drilling & Theory (15:15–15:42): 15:15–15:20 warm-up; 15:20–15:30 defensive positioning and marking; 15:30–15:42 interception, passing-lane awareness and controlled stick movement.

Games & Practical Application (15:42–16:10): 15:42–15:52 1-vs-1 defending challenge; 15:52–16:05 3-vs-3 attack-versus-defence game; 16:05–16:10 team defensive challenge.

Session outcome: Apply basic defensive positioning and attempt safe interceptions.

07Team Strategy & Game Awareness

Technical Drilling & Theory (15:15–15:42): 15:15–15:20 warm-up; 15:20–15:30 basic team shape and player positioning; 15:30–15:42 passing combinations, transitions and communication.

Games & Practical Application (15:42–16:10): 15:42–15:52 4-vs-2 keep-away game; 15:52–16:05 4-vs-4 tactical mini-match; 16:05–16:10 team challenge.

Session outcome: Understand simple team roles, communicate and make basic game decisions.

08Rules & Match Preparation

Technical Drilling & Theory (15:15–15:42): 15:15–15:20 warm-up; 15:20–15:30 rules, fouls, free hits, substitutions and sportsmanship; 15:30–15:42 match situations and referee signals.

Games & Practical Application (15:42–16:10): 15:42–15:52 rule-based floorball challenges; 15:52–16:05 supervised practice match; 16:05–16:10 match review.

Session outcome: Apply key safety rules and demonstrate fair play during a match.

09Mini Tournament & Competitive Play

Technical Drilling & Theory (15:15–15:42): 15:15–15:20 team warm-up; 15:20–15:30 tactical preparation and team assignments; 15:30–15:42 review of passing, shooting and match strategy.

Games & Practical Application (15:42–16:10): 15:42–16:05 mini floorball tournament using small-sided matches; 16:05–16:10 results and team feedback.

Session outcome: Use learned skills in a team competition and demonstrate positive sportsmanship.

10Final Assessment & Championship Day

Technical Drilling & Theory (15:15–15:42): 15:15–15:20 group warm-up; 15:20–15:30 individual skill assessment (dribbling, passing and shooting); 15:30–15:42 team strategy review and tournament preparation.

Games & Practical Application (15:42–16:10): 15:42–16:02 final floorball championship match; 16:02–16:07 fun skills competition; 16:07–16:10 closing, feedback and recognition.

Session outcome: Show progress in core skills and participate confidently in a supervised match.

Safety Guidelines

  1. Students must wear appropriate sportswear and non-marking indoor sports shoes suitable for the playing surface.
  2. Conduct a dynamic warm-up before technical drills and games.
  3. Keep the stick blade low and under control; no high or uncontrolled stick swings.
  4. No intentional body checking, pushing, tripping or other unsafe physical contact.
  5. Do not strike an opponent’s body or stick. Use controlled stick movements and maintain personal space.
  6. During shooting drills, students must shoot only when the lane is clear and must wait behind the designated line.
  7. Use only suitable floorball balls and inspect sticks, goals and playing surfaces before each session.
  8. Stop immediately when the coach or supervising staff blows the whistle.
  9. Use suitable protective equipment for goalkeeper activities; do not conduct goalkeeper drills without appropriate supervision and equipment.
  10. The coach must supervise all activities, adapt drills to students’ age and ability, and follow SIS safeguarding and emergency procedures.
  11. Students should report pain, injury, dizziness or feeling unwell to the coach immediately.

Training Methodology

  • Learning by Playing: Introduce skills through purposeful, enjoyable activities.
  • Progressive Skill Development: Move from simple individual tasks to partner, group and game situations.
  • Small-Sided Games: Use 1-vs-1, 2-vs-2, 3-vs-3 and 4-vs-4 formats to increase touches and participation.
  • Team Challenges: Use friendly competitions to build motivation and positive team spirit.
  • Practical Demonstrations: Coaches demonstrate each technique and explain key safety points before practice.
  • Positive Coaching: Encourage effort, confidence, creativity, inclusion and respect.
