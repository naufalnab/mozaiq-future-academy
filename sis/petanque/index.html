Program Overview

Petanque Academy is an extracurricular sports program introducing students to the skills and strategies of throwing metal boules as close as possible to the target ball (jack or cochonnet). Through structured skill practice, challenge-based activities, teamwork and friendly competition, students progress from basic techniques to a mini tournament.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the purpose, equipment, terminology and safety rules of Petanque.
  • Demonstrate the correct grip, stance, balance and basic underhand throwing motion.
  • Develop pointing skills to place a boule close to the jack while controlling direction and distance.
  • Understand the purpose of shooting and practise introductory shooting skills in a controlled environment.
  • Understand basic scoring, turn-taking, singles/doubles/triples formats and simple game strategies.
  • Demonstrate focus, patience, communication, teamwork and good sportsmanship.
  • Participate in a friendly mini tournament by applying the rules and skills learned.

Playing Area & Safety Guidelines

  • Minimum suggested practice area: approximately 4 × 8 metres for one basic practice lane and short-range targets.
  • Recommended area: approximately 5 × 10 metres for drills and varied fun games.
  • Ideal area: 6 × 12 metres or larger for multiple practice lanes and a mini tournament.
  • The surface should be stable, level, non-slippery and separated from other student traffic. Firm soil or fine gravel may be suitable when assessed as safe.
  • If a hard school surface is used, the coach must adapt throwing distance and intensity and secure the surrounding area.
  • Only the student whose turn it is may enter the throwing lane. Other students must wait behind the designated safety line.
  • Students must not retrieve boules or the jack until the coach confirms that the playing area is safe.
  • The coach must inspect the playing area and equipment and consider weather conditions before each session.

Expected Learning Outcomes

  • Students can identify Petanque equipment, follow safety rules and explain the basic flow of the game.
  • Students can perform an underhand throw and practise pointing at a level appropriate to their ability.
  • Students understand the purpose of shooting and can attempt the technique in controlled drills.
  • Students can calculate a basic score and take part in a beginner-level match.
  • Students demonstrate focus, patience, teamwork and sportsmanship.
  • Students participate in the mini tournament during Session 10.
