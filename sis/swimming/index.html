Program Overview

Mozaiq Swimming Academy is a structured extracurricular program designed to develop water confidence, aquatic safety awareness, fundamental swimming skills, physical fitness, self-confidence, teamwork, and sportsmanship. Instruction is progressive and adapted to each student’s age, readiness, and demonstrated swimming ability.

Each 55-minute session balances approximately 50% skill development and theory with 50% games and challenges. Games are always adapted to safe water depth and each participant’s ability. Completion of ten sessions does not guarantee independent swimming.

Safety Guidelines & Risk Management

  1. Before the program begins, SIS and MFA should confirm participant ages, swimming abilities, relevant support needs through approved school processes, pool depth, safe zones and emergency procedures.
  2. Assess each participant and group students by demonstrated ability. Beginners must remain in suitable shallow water and receive close, direct supervision.
  3. Confirm qualified lifeguard coverage and the facility emergency response plan for every session. Lifeguard coverage does not replace active coach supervision and headcounts.
  4. No running, pushing, dunking, breath-holding contests, rough play or unsupervised entry into the water.
  5. No diving, jumping or deep-water activity unless specifically permitted by the facility and supervised under an approved activity plan.
  6. Games must never require students to enter water beyond their demonstrated ability. Retrieval activities must use designated safe shallow zones.
  7. Conduct headcounts before entry, during transitions and after leaving the pool. Stop immediately when the coach or lifeguard gives the agreed signal.
  8. Check pool conditions and student readiness before each session. Stop participation if a student feels unwell, distressed, unusually cold, fatigued or unsafe.
  9. Keep first-aid access, emergency communication and the school/pool emergency action plan available and understood by staff.
  10. Use appropriate hydration, sun protection where relevant and safe deck footwear. Follow all SIS and pool-facility rules.
