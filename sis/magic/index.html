Program Overview

Mozaiq Magic Academy is an engaging extracurricular program that introduces students to the art of magic, illusion, and performance. Through age-appropriate activities using cards, coins, cups, ropes, silks, and other simple props, students learn how to create entertaining effects while developing confidence, creativity, communication, and critical thinking.

The program is designed as a practical, highly interactive learning experience. Each session combines a concise theory and instructor-demonstration block with guided practice, challenges, and performance games. The emphasis is on safe entertainment, respectful audience interaction, and responsible use of magic techniques.

Program Objectives

  • Introduce students to the fundamental principles of magic, illusion, and performance.
  • Develop creativity, imagination, focus, patience, and problem-solving skills.
  • Build confidence, public speaking, storytelling, and audience-engagement skills.
  • Improve hand-eye coordination and fine motor control through safe prop handling.
  • Encourage collaboration, positive feedback, and respectful performance etiquette.
  • Enable each student to prepare and present a short magic routine by the final session.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of the 10-session program, students should be able to:

  • Describe basic principles of magic, illusion, misdirection, and performance ethics.
  • Perform a selection of beginner-level effects using cards, coins, cups and balls, ropes, or visual props.
  • Demonstrate safe and responsible handling of all learning materials.
  • Create a simple magic routine with a clear opening, sequence, and closing.
  • Use voice, body language, storytelling, and audience interaction appropriately.
  • Give and receive constructive feedback and collaborate with peers.
  • Present a 2–3-minute individual or group performance at the final showcase.

Safety Guidelines & Code of Ethics

  • Only instructor-approved, age-appropriate props may be used.
  • No fire, pyrotechnics, dangerous chemicals, sharp blades, weapons, or hazardous stunts are permitted.
  • No tricks involving swallowing, choking, blindfolded movement, or placing objects in the mouth, nose, or ears.
  • Students must not throw props or perform tricks close to another person's face or body.
  • Use soft, lightweight, inspected props; damaged or unsuitable items must be removed immediately.
  • All practice involving props must take place under instructor supervision.
  • Students must respect personal space and obtain consent before any audience participation.
  • Mentalism activities must not pressure students to reveal private or sensitive personal information.
  • Magic is presented as entertainment and illusion; students must not claim supernatural powers or use tricks to deceive, intimidate, or embarrass others.
  • Props must be counted, checked, and returned safely at the end of each session.

Student Assessment Framework

Students are assessed through observation, practice tasks, peer interaction, and the final performance. Assessment should recognize progress, effort, safe practice, and confidence—not only technical perfection.

Final Showcase & Recognition

The final session may conclude with a small in-class showcase, subject to the school's approval and available space. Students may perform individually or in small groups. Recognition can celebrate a range of strengths and encourage participation without creating unnecessary pressure.

  • Best Young Magician
  • Most Creative Magic Trick
  • Best Stage Performance
  • Best Comedy Magician
  • Audience Favourite Magician
  • Best Team Performance
