🏹 Bina Bangsa School: CCA Archery Extracurricular Program
Target Audience: Middle School & High School
Duration: 1 Semester (12 Sessions)
Objective: To develop discipline, focus, and physical coordination through the art of archery, while maintaining a high level of engagement through a premium, gamified learning experience.

🌟 Premium Touches for Bina Bangsa School
To ensure the program meets the premium standards of Bina Bangsa School, the following elements are integrated into the curriculum:
Professional Equipment: Utilization of standardized recurve bows with varied draw weights suitable for teens (15-25 lbs), carbon arrows, and proper safety gear (arm guards, finger tabs, chest guards).
Digital Scoring Integration: During games and tournaments, an iPad or tablet connected to a TV/Monitor will display live scores, simulating an international competition environment.
Achievement Badges: Physical or digital pins awarded for milestones (e.g., "First Bullseye", "Robin Hood Try", "Flawless Posture").
Professional Coaching: Certified instructors trained to deliver clear, engaging instructions suitable for international standard students.

📅 12-Week Syllabus Overview
Week
Topic & Core Activity
🎯 Fun Game / Gamified Learning
Week 1
Introduction & Safety First: Equipment introduction, range safety rules, whistle commands, and basic eye dominance test.
Balloon Popper!



Shoot oversized balloons at a close distance to build early confidence and familiarity with the bow.
Week 2
The Foundation: Stance, posture, and the T-form. Practicing proper body alignment using stretch bands before handling actual bows.
Simon Says: Archery Edition



Instructor calls out fast posture commands. The last student to get into perfect form is out!
Week 3
Load & Draw: Proper nocking (loading the arrow onto the string) and drawing techniques without releasing (letting down safely).
Statue Challenge



Students hold the draw at full tension for as long as possible with proper form while the coach tries to distract them safely.
Week 4
Anchor & Aim: Finding the facial anchor point (corner of the mouth/under the chin) and learning to aim using the arrow tip or sight.
Archery Tic-Tac-Toe



Split into 2 teams. Students shoot at a 3x3 grid target to get 3 arrows in a row!
Week 5
Release & Follow-Through: Smoothly releasing the bowstring and holding the final pose until the arrow hits the target.
The Zombie Invasion



Targets are replaced with fun zombie posters. Hit the "brain" or "heart" for extra points!
Week 6
Mid-Semester Evaluation: Comprehensive review of all 5 basic steps. Individual form correction and one-on-one feedback.
Blind Partner Guide



One student is blindfolded and verbally guided by a partner to adjust their aim and hit a giant target.
Week 7
Grouping & Consistency: Focusing on muscle memory. Trying to shoot 3 arrows as close to each other as possible, regardless of where they hit.
Save the Royalty



Shoot the "ropes" (drawn lines) around a character on the target face without hitting the character.
Week 8
Speed & Agility: Learning how to nock, draw, and shoot safely and accurately within a strict time limit.
Relay Race Archery



Team sprint to the shooting line, safely shoot one arrow, and tag the next teammate.
Week 9
Distance Mastery: Stepping back! Increasing the target distance by 5 to 10 meters to challenge their aiming adjustments.
Artillery Strike



Aim for horizontal ground targets (hula hoops) using calculated parabolic/lob shots.
Week 10
Focus Under Pressure: Maintaining breathing, technique, and mental focus while facing external distractions.
Ambience Sound Arrows



Ambience Sound plays in the background. Students must time their release exactly when the Ambience Sound suddenly stops!
Week 11
Tournament Prep: Understanding international scoring systems, shooting line etiquette, and competitive match rules.
Bounty Hunter



Targets have hidden point values on the back (some negative!). Students gamble on which spot to hit.
Week 12
The Grand Finale: Bina Bangsa Archery Mini Tournament. A formal, competitive wrap-up to the semester.
King/Queen of the Range



Elimination-style tournament bracket to crown the best archer of the semester with a premium medal!


📊 Student Assessment System
Student grading focuses on safety, discipline, and technical progression rather than pure accuracy alone.
Weighting System
40% Technique & Posture: Formatively assessed weekly by the head coach.
30% Attitude & Safety: Adherence to range rules, whistle commands, and overall sportsmanship.
30% Accuracy & Performance: Summative scores taken from the Mid-Semester Evaluation (Week 6) and the Grand Finale Tournament (Week 12).
Archery Skill Rubric
Criteria
🌟 Premium (Exceeds Expectations)
✅ Competent (Meets Expectations)
🛠️ Novice (Needs Improvement)
Safety & Etiquette
Always follows rules, helps peers, and shows excellent range awareness.
Follows rules independently without needing constant reminders.
Needs frequent reminders about basic range safety and whistle commands.
Form & Posture
Flawless "T-form", steady stance, and consistent anchor point every single draw.
Good basic posture, but the anchor point occasionally shifts.
Unstable stance, struggles to find or hold a consistent anchor point.
Release & Follow-Through
Smooth release with a completely natural, relaxed, and held follow-through.
Decent release, but sometimes drops the bow arm too quickly after shooting.
"Plucking" the string aggressively or dropping the bow immediately.
Consistency (Grouping)
Arrows are tightly grouped together, consistently hitting the center zones.
Arrows hit the target consistently but are spread across various scoring zones.
Arrows frequently miss the target face entirely.


🎯 Program Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
These metrics will be used by the school administration and the coaching vendor to evaluate the success and premium quality of the program at the end of the semester.
1. Safety & Compliance
Zero Safety Incidents: Maintain a 100% incident-free and injury-free record throughout the entire 12-week semester.
Safety Mastery: 100% of enrolled students must pass the basic range safety quiz and practical demonstration by the end of Week 2.
2. Skill Progression
Target Consistency: By Week 6, 90% of the students can consistently shoot all their arrows onto the target face from a 5-meter distance.
Distance Achievement: By Week 10, 80% of the students successfully graduate and adapt to the 10-meter shooting line.
3. Engagement & Retention
High Attendance: Maintain an average student attendance rate of 85% or higher throughout the entire semester.
Student Satisfaction: Achieve an 8.5/10 or higher average score on the end-of-semester student feedback survey, with specific positive remarks on the gamification and "fun" aspect of the sessions.
4. Event Execution
Tournament Completion: Successfully execute the "Grand Finale" mini-tournament in Week 12, achieving 100% active participation from all attending students, complete with digital scoring and award distributions.
